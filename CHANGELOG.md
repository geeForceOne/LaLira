# Release notes

## Current version

### New
- Accounts with a currency, starting balance and opening date; money in and out with a running balance, and older years summed up as their year-end balance.
- Recurring bookings with templates such as "Deposit {month}", booked automatically and caught up after downtime.
- Monthly, quarterly and yearly email reports.
- CSV import and export, backups and restore.
- English and German, with region formats such as Swiss German.
