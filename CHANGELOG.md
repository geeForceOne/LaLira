# Release notes

## 1.5.0 - 2026-10-18

### New
- Account colors: each account has its own color, and the charts on the overview show every account as a band in it.
- A main currency: pick one in Settings to see everything converted into it, with daily exchange rates. A portfolio in several currencies can show its totals once in it.
- Price alerts for the shares in a portfolio, by email and on the overview.
- A portfolio's value over time: a chart with what you paid for it, and the gain per year.

### Improved
- Minor fixes and updates.

## 1.4.0 - 2026-10-16

### New
- Portfolios: record your purchases with date, quantity and price, sell all or part of them, and see value and gain or loss per currency.
- Current prices from EODHD with your own free API key, updated once a day.

## 1.3.0 - 2026-10-15

### New
- Each total on the overview has a balance chart (1 year, 5 years or everything).
- The small charts on the account cards show grid lines with their amounts.
- Dates can be typed as digits only: 29012016, 290116 or 2901 (this year), in your region's order.

## 1.2.0 - 2026-10-15

### New
- Sort your accounts on the overview: drag a card, or use the small arrows in its corners.
- "Only current year": a switch per account that hides older years from the list (search still finds them).
- The balance chart shows grid lines with their amounts.

### Improved
- Minor fixes and updates.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-10-14

### New
- An optional IBAN per account, checked for typos and shown with a copy button.
- Notes for positions, written in a small dialog and included in the CSV export and import.
- A balance chart in the account header (1 year, 5 years or everything).
- Search within an account, and expand or collapse all years at once.

### Improved
- Every backup also contains each account as a CSV file.
- Minor fixes and updates.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-14

Initial release.
